How to Use

  1. 1 Prep

    Start with clean, dry and oil-free skin. If applying lotion, make sure it is oil-free.

  2. 2 Apply

    Apply Body Coverage Perfector liberally using hands or a body brush, blending upward and outward. Let each layer dry for 10 minutes.

  3. 3 Perfect

    Buff away excess with a tissue, towel or mitt, then allow the final layer to set for 10 minutes before dressing.

FAQs

What makes Body Coverage Perfector different from other body makeup?

Flexible Tri-Effects Technology delivers buildable, natural-looking coverage that moves with skin.

Other body makeup feels heavy and cakey on my skin. Will Body Coverage Perfector feel like that?

No. The lightweight gel-pigment formula is designed to look and feel like skin.

Will Body Coverage Perfector transfer onto my clothes, sheets and furniture?

When applied to clean, oil-free skin, buffed and allowed to set for 10 minutes, it dries to a transfer-resistant finish.

I'm planning on wearing it outdoors. Can I wear sunscreen with it?

Yes. Let an oil-free sunscreen absorb completely before applying Body Coverage Perfector.

Can I use Body Coverage Perfector in the pool or ocean?

Yes. Once fully set, the formula is waterproof.

How do I remove Body Coverage Perfector?

Use a cleansing oil or body wash with a washcloth and warm water.

How can I find the right shade for my skin tone?

Use the shade finder, then select the shade closest to your natural skin tone.
